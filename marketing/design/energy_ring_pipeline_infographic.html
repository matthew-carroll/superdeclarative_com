STEP 1

128-Point Polar Sampling

Init / Resize
k = 0..127 (θ = 2πk/128)

Samples 128 uniform angles around a 2D circle. sin(θ) = +1 is closest to the viewer (bottom); sin(θ) = -1 is farthest away (top).

x₀ = cos(θ), y₀ = sin(θ)
STEP 2

3D Tilt & Foreshortening

Init / Resize
persp = 1 / (1 - 0.28·depth)

Tilts the ring into 3D (ry = rx · 0.22) and applies perspective division so the front arc widens toward the camera while the back arc narrows.

x = rx·cosT·persp, y = ry·sinT·persp
STEP 3

Unit Normals & Depth Taper

Geometry
w: 3.8px (front) → 1.1px (back)

Computes finite-difference tangent vectors and perpendicular outward unit normals (nx, ny), scaling base thickness by frontWeight.

baseW = 1.1 + ((sinT+1)/2) · 2.7
STEP 4

Traveling Wave Packets

Per Frame
5θ, 11θ, 19θ harmonics

Three traveling harmonic waves pulse local filament thickness (packetSurge) and jitter vertices along (nx, ny) so the beam arcs.

surge = 1 + .24w₁ + .14w₂ + .08w₃
STEP 5

3 Concentric Ribbon Paths

Per Frame
Corona 2.35× | Plasma 1× | Core .36×

Extrudes outer & inner boundaries along ±n for 3 nested SVG paths: Amber Corona (#FFB800), Yellow Plasma (#FFED4B), and White Core (#FFFDF2).

d = "M outer... Z M inner... Z"
STEP 6

Front-to-Back Depth Fade

SVG Gradient
α: 100% (0%) → 95% (45%) → 0% (72%)

A vertical <linearGradient> on each layer keeps the front 45% bright, then feathers alpha to 0 at 72% depth so the back 28% vanishes into fog.

y1="1" (front) → y2="0" (back)
STEP 7

Fractal Plasma & Dual Bloom

SVG Filters
feTurbulence + feGaussianBlur

Corona gets a dual Gaussian bloom (σ=2.2 + 6.5); main filament is warped & grained by 3-octave fractal noise whose frequency shifts every frame.

baseFreq = "0.042±.006 0.088±.012"
STEP 8

Neon Sputter & Light Sync

97% V
Final Composite + Icon Tint

neonInstability(t) triggers brief voltage dips every ~2s, flickering all 3 layers and modulating the golden sepia/saturate reflection on orbiting icons.

filter: brightness(V) sepia(.62)
