WebGL GPU Triangle-Strip & Multi-Pass Shader Pipeline (hero_rings.js + hero_beam.js)

How the Energy Rings Are Painted (Piece by Piece, Step by Step)

Instead of running CPU-bound SVG filters across the entire ring bounding box, the updated renderer builds a 258-vertex gl.TRIANGLE_STRIP and executes shaders only on the pixels along the beam—combining a 2-stage FBO separable Gaussian bloom with a single-pass GPU fractal noise + ragged-edge filament shader.

Phase A · CPU (hero_rings.js) Steps 1 – 2

Precomputes the 128-point perspective oval & tight visible bounding box (layOutBeam), then packs a 1,548-float Float32Array each frame with wave surges & normal jitter.

Phase B · WebGL Shaders (hero_beam.js) Steps 3 – 7

stripVertexShader extrudes the ribbon to ±(vHalf·uWidth + uPad). Offscreen FBOs render & blur the Tight (σ=2.2) and Wide (σ=6.5) Corona, then filamentFragmentShader paints the fractal-warped Plasma & Core in one pass.

Phase C · Composite & Light Step 8

Premultiplied alpha blending (ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA) composites the layers while neonInstability(t) flickers the beam and orbiting icons.

STEP 1

3D Oval & Clipped Canvas

Resize
73% depth box + 28px pad

computeRingBaseGeometry projects 128 points & normals (nx, ny). layOutBeam sizes the WebGL canvas tightly to the front 73% of the oval (plus 28px glow reach) snapped to device pixels.

top = front - (front-back)·0.73 - 28
STEP 2

Wave Packet Vertex Buffer

CPU → VBO
Float32Array((128+1)×2×6)

Every frame, 3 traveling waves (5θ, 11θ, 19θ) pulse halfPlasma and jitter (cx, cy). Writes two vertices per step (side = +1, -1) with 6 floats each into a preallocated VBO.

[cx, cy, nx, ny, halfPlasma, ±1]
STEP 3

GPU Triangle-Strip Extrusion

Vertex Shader
gl.drawArrays(TRIANGLE_STRIP, 258)

stripVertexShader extrudes the centerline along vNormal by (vHalf·uWidth + uPad)·side. Shaders only execute inside this narrow ribbon strip, skipping empty interior pixels.

vPos = aLine.xy + vNormal * vDistance
STEP 4

AA Coverage & Unblurred Glow

Glow Pass
uWidth = 2.35× | depthGradient

glowFragmentShader paints the unblurred amber corona (#FFB800 → #FF8A00) into FBO target 0, multiplying sub-pixel coverage() by depthGradient() (fading out at 72%).

cov = clamp(d+h+.5) - clamp(d-h+.5)
STEP 5

Dual Separable FBO Bloom

Blur + Corona
Tight σ=2.2 (≤2×) + Wide σ=6.5 (1×)

Blurs H then V in ping-pong FBOs using 10 bilinear-merged tap pairs (createBlurKernel). Wide blur runs at 1× CSS resolution for speed. coronaFragmentShader blends Tight over Wide.

(tight + wide * (1 - tight.a)) * uOpacity
STEP 6

256² Noise Lattice & Plasma

Filament Pass
3-Octave Perlin + Ragged Edge

Samples 256×256 uRedGreenLattice & uAlphaLattice textures across 3 octaves. Displaces vDistance by 2.2px, modulates alpha by grain, and layers quantized sharp pixels over a gaussianEdge soft blur.

plasma + soft * (1.0 - plasma.a)
STEP 7

Incandescent Core Fusion

Same Shader
0.36× Core + Plasma in 1 draw

In the exact same filamentFragmentShader invocation, computes the un-displaced hot white-gold core (#FFFDF2 → #FFF3A1 at 0.36× width) and composites it over the textured plasma.

core + plasma * (1.0 - core.a)
STEP 8

Final Blend & Light Sync

97% V
Corona + Filament + Icon Reflection

Hardware premultiplied blend (gl.ONE, gl.ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA) composites the Filament pass over the Corona pass. neonInstability(t) drives all 3 opacities and tints the orbiting icons & iPhone.

coreOp = .65 + (flicker - .85) * 2.2
